Approval queue

This is the two-person agreement step. Three kinds of action wait here until a different user approves them:

  • Rule overrides. Creating a tube that breaks a derivation rule (for example a library straight from native fluid, or a fraction not allowed for that specimen). The tubes are only created on approval.
  • Edits to existing tubes. Changes to status, quantity, location, parent, and so on are applied on approval. Creating tubes that pass the rules, recording custody hand-offs, attaching files, and QC imports (values read from an instrument file) do not need approval; they are audited.
  • Deleting or restoring a tube. Deletes are soft; the record and its aliquot number are kept.

The requester cannot approve their own request. Every approved action is stamped with the request number in the audit log. The scope of edit approvals is set by SAMPLE_EDIT_APPROVAL in config.py.
